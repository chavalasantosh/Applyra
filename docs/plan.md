# Applyra Intelligence Studio (100% White-Labeled Native Experience)

## 1. Overview & Vision
Applyra Intelligence Studio is **100% white-labeled as Applyra’s proprietary intelligence product**. 
End users must **never see where or how the data is gathered**, and there are **zero external redirect buttons** sending users away. Everything is presented under Applyra's brand:

1. **💼 Live Opportunities & Market CTC (Applyra Market Radar)**:
   - Real-time active roles with verified salary brackets and role specifications for any employer (ITC Infotech, TCS, Infosys, Google, etc.).
   - Filterable by **India Market** or **Global**.
   - Branded as `● APPLYRA MARKET RADAR`.

2. **📊 Compensation Distribution Benchmark (Applyra Benchmark Engine)**:
   - Verified market percentile breakdown, fixed base, performance bonuses, and equity compensation by level.
   - Internal third-party selectors are hidden (`hide_selector=true`) so the user only sees clean, native graphs.
   - Branded as `● APPLYRA BENCHMARK ENGINE`.

3. **🪜 Career Progression & Leveling (Applyra Leveling Matrix)**:
   - Standardized career progression and title equivalency mapping between peer employers.
   - Branded as `● APPLYRA LEVELING MATRIX`.

4. **📌 Your Pipeline Matching (Applyra Storage Sync)**:
   - Directly links to the candidate's actual opportunities stored in `chrome.storage.local`.
   - Branded as `● APPLYRA STORAGE SYNC`.

5. **🧮 Offer Simulator & Counter Strategy (Offer Calibration)**:
   - Fixed base, variable bonus, and equity calculations with strategic counter-offer targets.
   - Branded as `● OFFER CALIBRATION`.

---

## 2. White-Label & Security Rules
- **No external links**: Removed all "Open on [Website]" buttons. Users stay inside Applyra.
- **No third-party badges or attribution**: All badges use `APPLYRA` naming.
- **Clean Embedded Views**: `hide_selector=true` prevents third-party site headers and navigation menus from rendering.
- **Strict CSP Compliance**: Zero inline scripts; external files only (`script-src 'self'`).
